    ADS & PURCHASES

    Your progress, settings and event log stay on this device. Purchases are handled by Apple / Google (through RevenueCat, which tells the game which items you own). Ads are served as described below.

    This web test build keeps everything on your device: no ad SDK, no analytics SDK, and no network requests. Ads and purchases shown here are local test-mode previews of the real store flow.

    In the App Store / Play Store release, ads depend on the age setting you chose on first launch. Under 13: only non-personalised, contextual ads from Google AdMob (or none), no advertising ID, no tracking request. 13–15: non-personalised ads, nothing sold or shared. 16+: Google AdMob or AppLovin MAX may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse location and ad interactions, with consent where required (EEA/UK/CH via Google's consent form) and, on iPhone, App Tracking Transparency. Purchases are processed by Apple / Google; the game only learns which items you own.
